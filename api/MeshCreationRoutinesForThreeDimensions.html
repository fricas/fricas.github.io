MeshCreationRoutinesForThreeDimensions¶

mesh.spad line 1 [edit on github]

This package generates meshes for 3D plots.

meshPar2Var: (ThreeSpace DoubleFloat, (DoubleFloat, DoubleFloat) -> Point DoubleFloat, Segment DoubleFloat, Segment DoubleFloat, List DrawOption) -> ThreeSpace DoubleFloat

meshPar2Var(sp, f, s1, s2, l) adds a surface component to the space sp. The surface is built from curves obtained by evaluating f on a uniformly distributed rectangular lattice of points in the product of s1 and s2. Number of points of the lattice depends on list of options l.
